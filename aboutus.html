🌌 Our Mission

The Explorer’s Notebook is built to inspire curiosity, creativity, and action. We explore the universe and our planet through education, astronomy, and sustainability. From the stars above to the issues below, we make knowledge personal and powerful.



🧠 Who We Are

Founded by Anindya Ghosh, a developer and science communicator, The Explorer’s Notebook is a digital initiative focused on sharing meaningful stories through education. We’re not just content creators—we’re explorers.
